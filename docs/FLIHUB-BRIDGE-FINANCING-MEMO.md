# FliHub Bridge Financing Memo ($150,000)

**Status:** Draft for leadership review  
**Date:** 2026-09-14  
**Owner:** FLI Leadership / FliHub Executive Team

---

## 1) Purpose of the $150,000 Raise

FliHub is raising **$150,000** as a **separate near-term bridge round** to support a focused six-month execution period.  
This bridge is distinct from the larger FLI financing target of approximately **$7,000,000**.

### Bridge objectives
- Maintain product and team momentum
- Complete commercialization readiness work
- Generate early commercial traction (pilots/LOIs)
- Improve quality of diligence and fundraising position for the ~$7M financing

---

## 2) Investment Recipient Entity

### Preferred structure
Invest into a **dedicated FliHub legal entity** (or a newly formed wholly owned FLI subsidiary) to provide:
- Clean cap table treatment for bridge investors
- Clear investor narrative around FliHub-specific execution
- Better financial and legal traceability of use of proceeds

### Fallback structure (if preferred entity is not ready)
Invest into **FLI** with:
- Board-approved **ring-fenced use-of-proceeds**
- Written internal controls that dedicate bridge proceeds to FliHub-only scope
- Monthly bridge budget tracking and reporting

---

## 3) Investment Instrument and Investor Consideration

### Recommended instrument
Use a **Post-Money SAFE** for bridge efficiency and reduced transaction friction.

### What investors receive
- Right to convert into equity in the qualified financing (target: ~$7M raise)
- Standard information rights
- Optional pro-rata participation rights

### Term setting principle
Cap/discount terms should be set with legal counsel so bridge investors are appropriately rewarded while preserving flexibility and health of the future lead round.

---

## 5) Runway and Budget Discipline

- Target runway: **~6 months**
- Target average burn: **~$25,000/month**
- Budget control standard: bridge spending remains strictly scoped to this memo and monthly tracked.

---

## 6) Milestones Funded by the Bridge

By the end of the bridge period, FliHub should achieve:

1. **Commercialization package complete**
   - Pricing model
   - Offering/package definition
   - Customer onboarding flow
   - Pilot contract templates

2. **Early customer traction**
   - **3–5 qualified pilots or LOIs**

3. **Repeatable sales motion**
   - Initial pipeline framework
   - CRM operating cadence
   - Basic conversion KPI tracking

4. **Fundraising readiness artifacts**
   - Investor-grade data room
   - Updated 18–24 month model aligned to the ~$7M narrative

---

## 7) Relationship to the ~$7M FLI Raise

This bridge is an explicit **de-risking tranche** for the larger raise:
- Product readiness reduces technical and go-to-market risk
- Pilot/LOI traction strengthens market validation
- Cleaner diligence package improves investor confidence

The Post-Money SAFE is intended to convert in the qualified financing, linking bridge execution directly to the structure of the subsequent institutional round.

---

## 8) Required Investor Materials

Minimum package for bridge investor conversations:

1. Bridge one-pager (problem, product, why now, milestones, use of funds)
2. Instrument documents (SAFE and side letter if required)
3. Current and pro forma cap table
4. Financial model (base/upside/downside) + monthly bridge budget
5. Corporate/legal package:
   - Formation and governance records
   - IP ownership and assignments
   - Key contracts
   - Material risk disclosures

2. **Accounting/tax review** of structure and reporting treatment  
3. **CEO review and final approval** of terms, budget, and milestone plan
